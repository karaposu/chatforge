### P2 — Property-vs-stance belief distinction

**Question:** *How should the spec state the property-vs-stance belief distinction, explain why it accounts for the additive `f` vs multiplicative `g` combination structure, and provide a worked example?*

**Verification criteria:**
- [ ] Distinction stated: f-variables are *property-beliefs* about sender (what they are: Charm = high-status; Hope = future-positive; Fear = threat; Resonance = world-model match). g-modulators are *stance-beliefs* about sender (how they are: SP = agency-direction toward self; Coherence = stable mental model; EC = emotional regulation).
- [ ] Structural justification: property-beliefs sum independently because each property contributes to attachment-generation independently of the others. Stance-beliefs gate the product because any single stance-failure (incoherence read, emotional flooding read, supplication-display read) collapses attachment regardless of how favorable property-beliefs are.
- [ ] Worked example: a sender with high f-loadings (genuine Charm + Hope + Resonance) but a single stance-failure (Coherence collapse from contradictory behavior over time) generates attachment that decays — demonstrating multiplicative gating. Contrast: a sender with one strong f-loading (Resonance only — shared niche interest) and minimal others, with all g-modulators clean, generates real attachment — demonstrating additive sum.
- [ ] Connection to iteration-3.2.1's additive-`f` decision preserved: the distinction *explains* why additive-`f` was the right call — property-beliefs are the kind of belief that sums.
- [ ] Connection to iteration-3.2's multiplicative-`g` architecture preserved: the distinction *explains* why multiplicative gating is the right structure — stance-beliefs are the kind of belief that gates.
