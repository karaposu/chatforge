## Cycle 1 — Establish Hope's Structural Shape

### What was scanned

Before testing sub-flavor candidates, the structural shape of Hope itself must be precise. Iteration-3.4's belief-frame defines Hope as "the receiver's belief that interaction with the sender will yield future positive outcomes." Decomposing this into structural primitives:

- *Forward-oriented:* the belief is about a future state, not a current state.
- *Positive-valenced:* the future state is good for the receiver.
- *Sender-mediated:* the sender plays a causal role in producing the future state.
- *About the sender's effect on the receiver:* the future-positive happens TO or FOR the receiver, not to a third party.

### Distinguishing Hope from neighbor variables

- *Charm* — about what the sender IS now (status, competence, impressiveness). Not forward-oriented.
- *Fear* — forward-oriented but negative-valenced (threat).
- *Resonance* — about world-model match (current relational state). Not forward-oriented as primary.

### Hope's structural unifier

**Hope = the receiver's belief that the sender's future action, presence, or orientation will produce a specific kind of positive outcome for the receiver.**

This unifier is what makes Attention-Hope and Exchange-Hope both *count as Hope*: both are about the sender producing-something-good-for-me in the future. The "what kind of something good" is the discriminating axis (Object of Hope).

### Frontier state

**Advancing.** Hope's structural shape established. Test territory: which candidate sub-flavors fit this shape, which collapse into other variables, and what are the discriminating axes.

### Confidence map

- Hope's structural unifier: **Confirmed**.

### What was probed

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Forward-oriented | YES (future validation) |
| Positive-valenced | YES (being-seen-favorably) |

| Not Charm | YES (not about sender's status; about sender's view of receiver) |
| Not Fear | YES |
| Not Resonance | NEEDS PROBE — both involve the sender's understanding of receiver |
| PRAGMA-distinguishable | YES (signals like "you saw something in me," explicit recognition statements) |

### H_v vs Resonance probe

Resonance is dyadic-emergent (both parties' world-models align). H_v is asymmetric (the sender sees the receiver favorably). These are different operations:
- The sender can see the receiver favorably (high H_v) without sharing world-models — e.g., a parent who validates a child without understanding the child's deeper world.
- The sender can share world-models with the receiver (high Resonance) without validating the receiver — e.g., two people who think alike about politics, but the sender thinks the receiver is mediocre at their work.

**H_v PASSES** orthogonality test. Confirmed Hope sub-flavor distinct from Resonance.

### H_v vs H_a probe

H_a is "they will see me." H_v is "they will see me *favorably* (as I want to be seen)." H_v presupposes H_a (you can't validate without seeing) but adds valence about how the seeing is interpreted. Nested but distinct: H_a basic; H_v with positive valuation.

## Cycle 6 — Probe H_r (Reciprocity-Hope)

### What was probed

H_r: "this person will reciprocate my care / investment / give back what I give."

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Forward-oriented | YES (future return) |
| Positive-valenced | YES (return-on-investment) |
| Sender-mediated | YES (sender does the reciprocating) |
| Not Charm, Fear, Resonance | YES |
| Distinct from H_e | NEEDS PROBE |
| PRAGMA-distinguishable | YES (gratitude language, return-favor framing, debt/credit framing) |

### H_r vs H_e probe

H_e is "we will do things together (future joint exchange)." H_r is "the sender will give back what I have already given." Temporal asymmetry distinguishes them:
- H_e is symmetric in time: future exchange is open, both parties contribute prospectively.
- H_r is asymmetric in time: past investment by the receiver creates an expectation of future return by the sender.

Test case: a receiver who has invested heavily in the sender's project (gave time, energy, advice) and now wonders if the sender will repay the favor when needed. This is H_r — past investment, now hoping for future return — without specific H_e (no specific new joint activity in mind, just a sense that the sender is "good for it"). Conversely: a stranger met yesterday with whom the receiver hopes to collaborate — H_e high (future exchange), H_r near-zero (no past investment to be reciprocated).

**H_r PASSES** orthogonality test. Confirmed Hope sub-flavor distinct from H_e on temporal-conditional axis.

## Cycle 7 — Probe H_safe (Safety-Hope)

### What was probed

H_safe: "this person will protect me / be reliable when needed."

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Forward-oriented | YES (future protection) |
| Positive-valenced | YES (safety is positive) |
| Sender-mediated | YES (sender is protector) |
| Not Charm | NEEDS PROBE — Charm includes "competence" |
| Not Fear-absence | NEEDS PROBE — Fear-absence is sender-won't-harm |
| Not H_a, H_c | YES (different objects: protection vs attention vs presence) |
| PRAGMA-distinguishable | YES ("you've got my back," reliability language, protection framing) |

### H_safe vs Fear-absence probe

Fear is the receiver's belief that the sender CAN harm. Fear-absence is the belief that the sender WON'T harm. H_safe is the belief that the sender WILL ACTIVELY PROTECT.

Distinction: non-harm is passive (the sender does nothing bad); protection is active (the sender does something good, namely defend the receiver). H_safe is positive-valenced active hope; Fear-absence is null/neutral (no threat). They are distinct phenomena.

### H_safe vs Charm probe

Charm includes "competence/capability." If the sender is highly Charm-loaded for being a competent protector (e.g., a bodyguard with a known reputation), is that Charm or H_safe? Charm captures "the sender IS a competent protector" (current property); H_safe captures "the sender WILL deploy that competence to protect me when needed" (future-oriented). Different objects again — current state vs future deployment.

**H_safe PASSES** orthogonality test. Confirmed Hope sub-flavor.

### Frontier state

**Advancing.** Seven confirmed sub-flavors.

## Cycle 8 — Probe H_s (Status-Hope) — candidate from Seed 3

### What was probed

H_s: "this person will help me become higher-status / be associated with their status."

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Forward-oriented | YES |
| Positive-valenced | YES |
| Sender-mediated | YES |
| Not Charm | NEEDS HARD PROBE |
| PRAGMA-distinguishable | maybe — proximity-seeking, name-dropping signals |

### H_s vs Charm probe

Charm is the receiver's belief that the sender IS high-status. H_s is the receiver's hope that the sender will TRANSFER status to the receiver (via association, introduction, public acknowledgment).

Test: is H_s genuinely a distinct kind of Hope, or is it Charm-driven attachment with implicit status-transfer expectation?

- If the sender has high Charm and the receiver attaches partly because of expected status-association (being seen with the sender lifts the receiver's status), is the attachment driven by Charm alone, or by Charm + a separate H_s?
- Probe: imagine a sender with zero Charm (no status, no competence, not impressive) but who somehow could elevate the receiver's status via introduction. Does the receiver have H_s? In practice, this configuration is rare — status-transfer requires the sender to have status to transfer, which is exactly what Charm captures. H_s without Charm is structurally near-impossible.
- Alternative: status-transfer expectation, when present, is either (a) a specific instance of H_e (the future exchange is "introduce me to your circle") or (b) a Charm-amplification effect (Charm-loaded sender is hoped to confer some Charm onto receiver via association — this is in attachment to the SENDER, not a separate Hope sub-flavor).

**H_s FAILS** orthogonality test. Status-elevation expectation collapses into Charm + (optionally) H_e specialized to introduction-exchange. Not a distinct Hope sub-flavor.

*Seed extracted from H_s kill:* "status-related future expectations are a hybrid of Charm-driven attachment and exchange-Hope specialized to status-transfer. PRAGMA may detect status-transfer-flavored interactions but should classify them as Charm + H_e_specialized rather than as their own sub-flavor."

**Advancing — but with kill.** Seven confirmed; one rejected.

## Cycle 10 — Identify Discriminating Axes

### What was scanned

With seven confirmed sub-flavors, what axes discriminate them from each other?

### Axis 1 — Object of Hope (load-bearing primary)

The core discriminating axis. What kind of positive outcome is hoped for?

| Sub-flavor | Object |
|---|---|
| H_e | Activity / exchange (what we do together) |
| H_a | Attention (sender sees me specifically) |
| H_v | Validation (sender sees me favorably) |
| H_c | Presence (sender stays around) |
| H_g | Growth (receiver becomes more through interaction) |
| H_r | Reciprocity (sender returns past investment) |
| H_safe | Protection (sender actively protects/is reliable) |

Object-of-Hope is the primary discriminator. Each sub-flavor names a distinct kind of positive outcome.

### Axis 2 — Agent of Fulfillment (load-bearing secondary)

Most sub-flavors have sender as primary agent. Two have receiver as co-agent or primary agent:
- *Sender-primary:* H_a, H_v, H_c, H_safe (sender does the seeing / staying / protecting)
- *Co-agent:* H_e, H_r (both parties contribute to exchange/reciprocity)
- *Receiver-primary:* H_g (receiver does the growing; sender catalyzes)

This axis matters for one distinction (H_g's catalyst-role vs other sub-flavors' sender-primary-role) but does not orthogonally discriminate beyond that.

### Axis 3 — Temporal Conditioning (load-bearing for H_r)

Most sub-flavors are time-symmetric (open-ended future). H_r is uniquely past-conditioned (requires past investment by receiver). This axis discriminates H_r from H_e (the most likely confusion).

### Axis 4 — Dependence on Other Sub-flavors

Some sub-flavors are nested:
- H_v presupposes H_a (you must see-at-all to see-favorably)
- H_a presupposes H_c (you must be-around to attend)
- H_safe presupposes H_c (you must be-around to protect)

These nestings are real but do not collapse the sub-flavors — each adds a distinct positive valence beyond its predecessor.

### Axes that are NOT load-bearing

- *Time horizon (near vs far):* a receiver can have any sub-flavor near-term or far-term. Same sub-flavor, different horizons.
- *Specificity to this person:* all Hope sub-flavors are about THIS sender (otherwise generic future-hope, not a Hope variable about a specific person).
- *Asymmetry of fulfillment:* maps onto Object-of-Hope (active objects like exchange require receiver participation; passive objects like presence don't).

### Frontier state

**Stable.** Discriminating axes mapped. Object-of-Hope is the primary axis.

## Cycle 11 — Architectural Status Check

### What was probed

Should any sub-flavor be split out as a separate `f`-variable, expanding the variable count beyond four?

### Orthogonality test for sub-flavor variable-split

The orthogonality test from iteration-3.2.1 requires: a new variable must (a) generate attachment alone (with all others at zero), (b) be a distinct character not reducible to existing variables, (c) be orthogonal to existing variables.

**Test on H_a (the iteration-3.3 Refinement 3 case):**

Can H_a generate attachment alone — with H_e, H_v, H_c, H_g, H_r, H_safe ALL at zero, AND with Charm, Fear, Resonance also at zero?

Probe: a stranger briefly looks at you intently in passing. No Charm (you don't know them). No Hope-of-future (just a passing moment). Some H_a (they saw you specifically). Without Charm or other variables, attachment? Generally no — you might feel noticed but not attached.

H_a alone does not produce meaningful attachment. It is not orthogonal to the rest of `f`.

**Same probe for other sub-flavors:** each individual sub-flavor, in isolation from the rest of `f`, fails to produce attachment. Sub-flavors are not orthogonal.

### Hope as a whole DOES pass orthogonality

iteration-3.2 confirmed that Hope as a whole generates attachment alone (e.g., a transactional exchange offer with no Charm, Fear, or Resonance). What passes orthogonality is the sum of sub-flavors, not any individual sub-flavor.

### Conclusion

Hope sub-flavors are **partition-within-Hope**, not separate variables. Hypothesis Alpha confirmed (with Beta correction: H_s and H_pleasure collapsed). Hypothesis Gamma (variable split) ruled out — no sub-flavor passes individual orthogonality.

### Architectural status

The sub-taxonomy ships as content within Hope's specification. No formula change. Iteration-label classification: clarification within iteration-3.4 (the belief-frame iteration) → iteration-3.4.1 OR sub-content within iteration-3.4 if integrated before 3.4 ships in spec.

## Cycle 12 — Test for Cross-Sub-flavor Linear Combinations

### What was probed

Most concrete Hope-instances are mixtures, not pure single-sub-flavor instances. Test whether the sub-flavors function as a basis (combinations possible) or as exclusive categories (each instance assigned one).

### Test cases

**Case 1 — "I hope this person will love me"**: This is a multi-sub-flavor instance:
- H_a (continued specific attention) +
- H_v (sees me favorably) +
- H_c (will stay) +
- H_safe (will protect) +
- often H_e (joint life activities) and H_g (will help me become more)

Love-hope is a high-dimensional vector across many sub-flavors.

**Case 2 — "I hope this person will tell me the truth"**: Hybrid:
- H_v (sees me as someone worth telling truth to) +
- H_safe (reliability)

**Case 3 — "I hope this person will be alive next year"**: Predominantly H_c, possibly with affective overlay (sadness if they won't be, but that's Fear, not Hope).

**Case 4 — "I hope this person responds to my message"**: Pure H_a (specific attention to me) plus possibly H_e (engage in exchange).

### Conclusion

Hope sub-flavors function as a **basis**, not an exclusive partition. A Hope-instance is a vector with components on multiple sub-flavors. PRAGMA detection should report sub-flavor distribution, not single classification.

### Frontier state

**Advancing slightly.** Basis-vs-partition distinction surfaced.

## Cycle 13 — Jump Scan: Hope-like phenomena that don't fit any sub-flavor

### Direction: deliberately different

Scan in regions not previously considered: are there forward-positive-sender-mediated beliefs that don't fit any of the seven confirmed sub-flavors?

### Tested

- *Hope of vicarious experience* — "I hope this person will tell me about their adventures so I can experience them through them." Possible new sub-flavor? No — collapses into H_e (specific exchange = storytelling) and possibly H_g (growth via vicarious experience).
- *Hope of sponsorship* — "I hope this person will publicly back me." Collapses into H_e (specific exchange = endorsement) and partially H_v (validation through public backing).
- *Hope of inheritance* — "I hope this person will leave me something." Collapses into H_e (future material exchange) with strong temporal-far-horizon component.
- *Hope of forgiveness* — "I hope this person will forgive me for what I did." This is interesting. It's forward-oriented, positive-valenced, sender-mediated. It doesn't fit H_e (forgiveness isn't an exchange in the joint-activity sense), H_a (it's specifically about being-forgiven, not generic attention), H_v (forgiveness is acceptance-despite-fault, which is not the same as positive-validation of self-image), H_c, H_g, H_r, or H_safe. Could H_forgive be a missed sub-flavor?

**Probe H_forgive further.** "Hope of forgiveness" requires past wrongdoing by the receiver. This makes it a special temporally-conditioned sub-flavor like H_r (which is past-investment-conditioned). H_r is past-positive-investment hoping for future-positive-return; H_forgive is past-negative-action hoping for future-positive-acceptance. Different sign on the past condition, similar structure.

Is H_forgive distinct from H_v? H_v is "they will see me as I want to be seen" (favorable view). Forgiveness is not exactly favorable view — it's acceptance-despite-fault, which presupposes the sender currently sees the receiver in a negative light (because of the wrongdoing) and hopes for that to soften. So H_forgive ≠ H_v.

Is H_forgive its own sub-flavor? It's narrow (only applies in post-wrongdoing contexts) and may collapse into H_v + H_c (continued availability despite wrongdoing). Or into H_safe (protection from the consequences of wrongdoing).

Most parsimonious classification: H_forgive is a hybrid of H_v + H_c with a past-negative-condition modifier, similar to how H_r is a hybrid case of exchange with past-positive-condition. Not a distinct primary sub-flavor.

But this surfaces an interesting structural pattern: past-conditioning is a real axis (Axis 3 from Cycle 10) that produces hybrid sub-flavors — H_r (past-positive) and possibly H_forgive (past-negative, hybrid).

### Jump scan finding

No new primary sub-flavor discovered. The seven confirmed sub-flavors plus past-conditioning hybrids (H_r and H_forgive-style cases) cover the territory. H_forgive does not pass distinct-primary-sub-flavor test because it is a hybrid; it is documented as a recognized hybrid case in PRAGMA detection.

### Frontier state

**Stable.** Jump scan complete; no uncharted voids of structural significance.
