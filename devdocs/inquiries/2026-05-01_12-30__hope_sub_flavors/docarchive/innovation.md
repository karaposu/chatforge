### P1 — Foundation: Hope's structural unifier + axes + external grounding

#### P1-A (generic) — "Minimum-sufficient unifier statement + axes + one-paragraph grounding" [Constraint Manipulation]

**Mechanism:** Constraint Manipulation. Constraint: stay vocabulary-only; smallest sufficient statement.

**Output:**

*Hope's structural unifier:*
> "Hope is the receiver's belief that the sender's future action, presence, or orientation will produce a specific kind of positive outcome for the receiver. Hope shares with all attachment variables the property of being a receiver-side belief about the sender, and it is distinguished from the others by being forward-oriented (about future state), positive-valenced (good outcome expected), sender-mediated (sender plays a causal role), and receiver-affecting (the future-positive happens TO or FOR the receiver). Charm is about what the sender IS now (current properties, not future state). Fear is forward-oriented but negative-valenced (threat). Resonance is about world-model match (current relational state)."

*Discriminating axes:*
- *Primary axis — Object of Hope:* what kind of positive outcome is hoped for. The seven sub-flavors below partition along this axis.
- *Secondary axis — Agent of fulfillment:* most sub-flavors have the sender as primary agent; some are co-agent (H_e, H_r) or receiver-primary (H_g).
- *For specific cases — Temporal conditioning:* H_r is past-positive-conditioned; some hybrid cases (H_forgive-style) are past-negative-conditioned.
- *Axes that are NOT load-bearing:* time horizon (a sub-flavor can be near-term or far-term — same sub-flavor, different horizons); specificity-to-this-person (all Hope sub-flavors are about this specific sender); asymmetry-of-fulfillment (maps onto Object-of-Hope).

*External vocabulary anchor:*
> "The forward-positive-belief structure that defines Hope draws on belief-formation cognitive theory broadly construed (Bayesian belief-update, schema theory, Bowlby's working models). Vocabulary-only anchor; no apparatus commitment to any specific tradition."

#### P1-B (focused) — "Unifier + axes + grounding unified into argumentative arc" [Combination]

**Mechanism:** Combination. Three sub-elements unified into one section that flows from "what makes something Hope" through "how sub-flavors differ" to "where the structural shape comes from."

**Output:** A single ~3-paragraph section ("The Structural Shape of Hope") that opens with the unifier, follows with axes that derive directly from the unifier (Object-of-Hope is "what kind of positive outcome"; Agent and Temporal are derived modulations), and closes with the external grounding that anchors "belief" to external cognitive theory. Each paragraph carries the previous paragraph's terms forward.

#### P1-C (contrarian) — "Lead with sub-flavors; derive unifier inductively" [Inversion]

**Mechanism:** Inversion. Reverse the order: present the seven sub-flavors first, then ask what they share, then state the unifier as the inductive answer.

**Output:** "Looking at the seven sub-flavors of Hope (H_e, H_a, H_v, H_c, H_g, H_r, H_safe), what do they share? Each is forward-oriented, positive-valenced, sender-mediated, and receiver-affecting. That commonality IS Hope's structural unifier."

**Inversion depth check:** First-level. Going deeper: maybe the sub-flavors don't determine the unifier — maybe the unifier is independently necessary for *defining what counts as a sub-flavor*. The deeper inversion converges back to unifier-first.

### P2 — Primary tier: H_e, H_a, H_v, H_c

#### P2-A (generic) — "Per-sub-flavor template applied four times" [Combination]

**Mechanism:** Combination. Definition + vignette + PRAGMA signals template applied uniformly to four sub-flavors.

**Output:**

**H_e (Exchange-Hope) — primary**
- *Definition:* the receiver's belief that the sender's future action will yield positive joint exchange — collaboration, shared activity, dating, friendship-engagement.
- *Vignette:* iteration-3.3's Person A Reddit message ("saw your product post, fricking good idea, really appreciate it — let's meet to see if we can collaborate"). Person A's "let's meet to collaborate" is concrete H_e: a specific future joint exchange offered.
- *PRAGMA signals:* concrete-offer language ("let's do X"); plan-language ("on Thursday / next month"); specific-activity references; named joint-action proposals.

**H_a (Attention-Hope) — primary**
- *Definition:* the receiver's belief that the sender will continue to attend to them specifically — see them, notice them, direct attention at them.
- *Vignette:* iteration-3.3 Refinement 3's nightclub case. A man giving one woman extended specific attention from his own evident enjoyment of the moment (selective-engagement-mode g₁) realizes high H_a in her: she experiences "this person sees me, and will keep seeing me." Contrast: the same man visibly occupied with his own conversation, giving no specific signal of interest, suppresses H_a regardless of his other f-loadings.
- *PRAGMA signals:* selective-engagement-mode markers from iteration-3.3 R3 (named-counterparty references, expressed evaluation, specific attention-direction).

**H_v (Validation-Hope) — primary**
- *Definition:* the receiver's belief that the sender will see them as they want to be seen — recognize their self-image, validate their self-presentation.
- *Vignette:* a relationship where Resonance is high but H_v is low. Two colleagues think alike about politics (high Resonance — shared world-models on political issues) but the sender thinks the receiver is mediocre at their work and has not signaled otherwise. The receiver experiences low H_v despite high Resonance: shared world-models do not entail favorable view of the receiver.
- *PRAGMA signals:* explicit-recognition statements ("you saw something in me"); self-image-affirming signals; specific compliments tied to the receiver's self-concept; absence of unsolicited critique.

**H_c (Continuity-Hope) — primary**
- *Definition:* the receiver's belief that the sender will continue to be present — stay in their life, remain reachable.
- *Vignette:* an estranged family member case where H_c is present without H_a. The receiver's parent stays alive, findable, and would respond if contacted — but does not direct specific attention at the receiver. The receiver has high H_c (parent stays around) and low H_a (parent doesn't attend specifically). Demonstrates H_c's distinction from H_a.
- *PRAGMA signals:* commitment statements ("I'll be here"); reliability signals; explicit availability framing; consistency-over-time markers.

### P3 — Secondary tier: H_g, H_r, H_safe

#### P3-A (generic) — "Context-of-relevance lens" [Lens Shifting]

**Mechanism:** Lens Shifting. View each secondary sub-flavor under a "context-of-relevance" lens — present each not abstractly but with explicit relationship-context where it becomes operationally important.

**Output:**

**H_g (Generative-Hope) — secondary, mentorship/development context**
- *Definition:* the receiver's belief that the sender's continued mentorship, challenge, or example will help the receiver grow.
- *Context-of-relevance:* mentorship relationships (formal — student/mentor; informal — admired figure who teaches by example); developmental relationships (therapist/client where growth is goal-oriented); family relationships where one party is in a "becoming" phase.
- *Distinctive feature:* receiver-primary agency. The receiver does the growing; the sender catalyzes.
- *PRAGMA signals:* mentorship-language; challenge framing; learning-together signals; growth-references.

**H_r (Reciprocity-Hope) — secondary, long-investment context**
- *Definition:* the receiver's belief that the sender will return what the receiver has already given — past investment will produce future return.
- *Context-of-relevance:* long-friendship relationships where the receiver has invested years of care; professional relationships where the receiver has supported the sender's projects; family relationships with cumulative caregiving.
- *Distinctive feature:* past-conditioned. Requires past investment by receiver to be meaningful.
- *PRAGMA signals:* gratitude-language; return-favor framing; debt/credit framing; past-investment references.

**H_safe (Safety-Hope) — secondary, protection-relevant context**
- *Definition:* the receiver's belief that the sender will actively protect them or be reliable when needed — not merely will-not-harm but will-actively-deploy-capability-on-receiver's-behalf.
- *Context-of-relevance:* parent-child relationships; relationships with explicit protector-roles (mentor, sponsor, ally); relationships in high-threat environments where reliability matters.
- *Distinctive feature:* active protection (vs Fear-absence which is null/passive).
- *PRAGMA signals:* "you've got my back"; reliability statements; protection framing; deployment-of-capability signals.

#### P3-B (focused) — "Merged 'Secondary recognized sub-flavors' subsection" [Combination]

**Mechanism:** Combination. Combine the three secondary sub-flavors into a single subsection that emphasizes their shared context-specific status.

**Output:** A single section titled "Secondary Recognized Sub-flavors" that opens with: "Three additional Hope sub-flavors are recognized as operationally important in specific relationship contexts but are not primary in most attachment scenarios. They share with primary sub-flavors the structural shape of Hope (forward + positive + sender-mediated + receiver-affecting); they differ in operational frequency."

Then briefer treatment per sub-flavor (one paragraph each) as in P3-A but more compressed.

#### P3-C (contrarian) — "Promote H_g to primary; demote H_v" [Inversion]

**Mechanism:** Inversion. Challenge the tier composition — what if H_g should be primary and one of the four (H_v?) should be secondary?

**Output:** "H_g (growth through interaction) appears in many relationships beyond mentorship — it's a fundamental relational hope ('this relationship will make me better'). Meanwhile H_v (favorable view) might be more context-specific than tier composition suggests; many attachment scenarios have low H_v salience (e.g., transactional collaborations have H_e dominant without strong H_v). Reconfigure: primary = {H_e, H_a, H_c, H_g}; secondary = {H_v, H_r, H_safe}."

**Inversion depth check:** First-level. The claim that H_g is broadly applicable has merit, but H_v's frequency in romantic, friendship, and recognition-relevant relationships is also broad. Sensemaking's tier composition was based on operational frequency across attachment scenarios broadly, not just mentorship. The contrarian's reconfiguration is plausible but doesn't decisively override the existing tier composition.

### P4 — Architectural framing: basis-decomposition + grounding + PRAGMA + nesting + killed

#### P4-A (generic) — "All five atoms in one section" [Combination]

**Mechanism:** Combination. Five content atoms combined into a single "Architectural Framing of the Sub-Taxonomy" section.

**Output:** A section that opens with the basis-decomposition statement, follows with external grounding (linear-combination vocabulary), then PRAGMA implementation note, then sub-flavor nesting documentation, then killed candidates documentation. Each atom is one paragraph.

*Basis-decomposition statement:*
> "Hope sub-flavors function as a basis for Hope-instances. A concrete Hope-instance is a linear combination across the basis sub-flavors, not an exclusive single classification. Example: 'I hope this person will love me' has H_a + H_v + H_c + H_safe + sometimes H_e + H_g all simultaneously."

*External grounding:*
> "The basis-decomposition pattern draws on linear-combination vocabulary from feature decomposition / factor analysis. APT does not commit to any specific apparatus (e.g., it does not commit to PCA-style orthogonal decomposition or to factor-analytic rotation); the structural claim is that Hope-instances admit decomposition along the basis of named sub-flavors."

*PRAGMA implementation note:*
> "PRAGMA's Hope detection extends to maintain per-sub-flavor parallel detection. Output is a weight vector across sub-flavors, not a single classification. Operational cost: medium (per-sub-flavor classifier development + weight aggregation). Several detectors leverage existing PRAGMA capability (template-vs-specific from iteration-3.2.1; selective-engagement-mode from iteration-3.3 R3)."

*Sub-flavor nesting documentation:*
> "Three nesting relations exist among sub-flavors: H_v presupposes H_a (you must see-at-all to see-favorably); H_a presupposes H_c (you must be-around to attend); H_safe presupposes H_c (you must be-around to protect). Nesting is real but does not collapse the sub-flavors — each adds distinct positive valence beyond its predecessor. PRAGMA's basis decomposition handles dependent components: a non-zero V-weight implies non-zero A-weight, etc."

*Killed candidates documentation:*
> "Two candidate sub-flavors were tested and rejected. (1) H_s (Status-Hope — 'sender will help me become higher-status') collapses into Charm + H_e specialized to introduction-exchange; PRAGMA classifies status-related future expectations as Charm + H_e_specialized. (2) H_pleasure (Pleasure-Hope — 'sender will be enjoyable to be around') is a cross-variable affective specification, not a sub-flavor; PRAGMA classifies pleasure/enjoyment by the variable carrying the pleasure (H_e, Charm, or Resonance depending on framing). Hybrid cases like H_forgive (past-negative-conditioned 'sender will accept me despite wrongdoing') are documented as recognized hybrids of H_v + H_c, not primary sub-flavors."

#### P4-B (focused) — "Feature-decomposition / factor-analysis vocabulary anchor" [Domain Transfer]

**Mechanism:** Domain Transfer from feature decomposition / factor analysis traditions.

**Output:** Import the structural primitives of feature decomposition / factor analysis as vocabulary anchors:
- *Basis vector* = each sub-flavor (H_e, H_a, H_v, H_c, H_g, H_r, H_safe)
- *Weight* = the magnitude of each basis sub-flavor in a Hope-instance
- *Linear combination* = the structural claim about how a Hope-instance decomposes
- *Rotation* (informal note) = the choice of basis is not unique; alternative tier compositions or equivalent sub-flavor namings are possible if they decompose the same Hope-instance space
- *Independence* (informal note) = the seven sub-flavors are not strictly orthogonal in the linear-algebra sense (nesting relations exist) but they are *category-distinct* per the orthogonality test

The vocabulary connects APT's basis-decomposition claim to a recognizable formal structure for readers from quantitative cognitive science / data analysis backgrounds. Vocabulary-only anchor; no apparatus commitment (APT does not commit to PCA, factor rotation, etc., as canonical methods for Hope-decomposition).

#### P4-C (contrarian) — "Reject basis-decomposition; ship as exclusive partition" [Inversion]

**Mechanism:** Inversion. Challenge sensemaking's basis-vs-partition decision.

**Output:** "Sub-flavors as exclusive categories (each Hope-instance has one dominant classification) is simpler to teach, easier for PRAGMA to implement (single-classification output), and matches typical taxonomic presentation in psychology. Shipping as basis-decomposition adds bookkeeping cost without operational benefit — the dominant sub-flavor in any given case is what matters, not the full distribution."

**Inversion depth check:** First-level. Sensemaking Ambiguity 3 already addressed this: empirical structure of concrete Hope-instances (a love-hope is genuinely multi-sub-flavor, not just dominantly-one) plus external mathematical grounding (linear-combination decomposition is standard) both favor basis. The contrarian's "single-classification simpler" is presentation aesthetics; the basis claim is structural content.
